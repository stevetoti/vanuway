# VanuWay Launch Audio Direction

## Voiceover

Style: live, premium advertising narration. Warm, confident, modern, and inviting. The voice should sound like a real launch announcement for Vanuatu, not a tutorial and not an overly dramatic movie trailer.

Delivery:
- Natural smile in the voice.
- Medium pace, clear enough for mobile listening.
- Slight lift on service lists, but no rushed checklist feeling.
- Strong closing emphasis on the call to action.
- Read service groups like confident benefits, not a feature dump.
- Leave tiny breath space after "Vanuatu, meet VanuWay" and before the final web address.
- The final line should feel direct and memorable, like a campaign sign-off.

Closing line:
Visit www.vanuway.com and download the app from there.

## Music Bed

Style: cinematic island-tech launch bed.

Mood:
- Warm, inspiring, forward-moving.
- Light Pacific lifestyle energy with modern digital confidence.
- Should feel like a national app launch, not nightclub music.

Instrumentation direction:
- Soft cinematic pad under the whole spot.
- Subtle low pulse for movement and confidence.
- Light airy texture for ocean/island atmosphere.
- Optional gentle percussive hits for scene transitions.

Mix:
- Voiceover must remain dominant.
- Music should sit low under narration, around 10-15% perceived loudness.
- Duck music slightly under important service-list lines.
- Add soft whooshes for app-screen scrolling and scene changes.
- Fade the music in during the first two seconds.
- Build slightly under the business-dashboard section, then simplify for the closing call to action.
- End with a short clean brand sting after the web address, without covering the last word.

## Sound Effects

Use sparingly:
- Soft phone tap when a real app screen appears.
- Subtle scroll sound for animated app screens.
- Gentle transition whoosh between service groups.
- Final clean logo sting under the call to action.
- Very light low whoosh on each major feature change.
- Keep effects quieter than the voice and music; they should add motion, not call attention to themselves.

Avoid:
- Cartoon UI sounds.
- Loud impacts.
- Overly busy music that competes with the service list.
- Robotic or artificial voice pacing.
