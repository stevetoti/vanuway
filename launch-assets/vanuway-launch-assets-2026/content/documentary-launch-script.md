# VanuWay Documentary Launch Script

## Production Voiceover

There is a place in the Pacific where life moves between islands, villages, markets, airports, ports, homes, hotels, businesses, and communities.

For the people of Vanuatu, movement is family. It is work. It is trade. It is tourism. It is opportunity.

And now, Vanuatu has one digital home built for all of it.

This is VanuWay.

For Ni-Vanuatu families, VanuWay makes everyday life easier. Book a ride, pre-book an airport or cruise transfer, send a package, order food, shop from local stores, and buy or sell through the marketplace.

For visitors arriving by cruise ship, VanuWay becomes a guide from the moment they step onto the island. Find safe transport, book tours and attractions, discover community events, shop from local businesses, and learn useful Bislama phrases.

For travellers arriving by flight, VanuWay connects the airport to the rest of the journey. Check arrivals, book transfers, find hotels, plan ferry routes, and explore domestic flight options between islands.

For expats coming to live, work, or invest in Vanuatu, VanuWay helps them settle faster. Find real estate, connect with service providers, access hospitals, pharmacies, and labs through VanuHealth, and discover jobs, freelancing, and hiring through VanuJobs.

For local businesses, VanuWay opens a new digital door. Restaurants, shops, hotels, tour operators, ferry operators, marketplace sellers, service providers, health providers, property agents, event organizers, and employers can reach customers from one platform.

Customers and businesses can message inside VanuWay, manage orders and listings, promote services, review activity, and keep every conversation closer to the transaction.

VanuWay is also built for community. With VanuWay Daily, people can check weather, earthquake updates, tsunami alerts, currency information, kava prices, water taxi routes, power outage updates, and emergency numbers.

With emergency alerts, people stay informed when safety matters most. With community events, locals and visitors discover what is happening around them. With Learn Bislama, visitors, expats, and younger generations can connect more deeply with Vanuatu's national language.

This is not just another app.

It is a digital bridge between people, businesses, islands, and opportunity.

A ride to work. A meal delivered home. A package sent across town. A hotel booked before arrival. A tour reserved from a cruise ship. A job opportunity found. A small business discovered. A health service reached at the right moment.

That is the power of VanuWay.

Built for Vanuatu. Built for Ni-Vanuatu. Built for visitors, expats, local businesses, drivers, vendors, families, and communities across the islands.

One platform. Many services. One Vanuatu, more connected.

Visit www.vanuway.com and download or open the VanuWay app from there.

VanuWay. Your way around Vanuatu.
